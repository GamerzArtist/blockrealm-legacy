## 2026-07-30 - Theme-aligned Keyboard Accessibility with Focus-Visible Styles
**Learning:** Stylized custom inputs and buttons (e.g. Minecraft/retro block style with custom borders) often use `outline: none` or custom focus handling that completely strips away native keyboard focus outlines. This renders keyboard-only or screen-reader users completely blind to their current interactive selection. Using `:focus-visible` with a bright, theme-appropriate color (like the Minecraft-green `#70b85a` used in our app) and an offset restores full interactive clarity without compromising the game's retro styling for mouse/touch-only users.
**Action:** When designing customized retro UI/UX components, always ensure a distinct `:focus-visible` style is defined using brand colors and outlines to preserve accessibility for keyboard navigation.
